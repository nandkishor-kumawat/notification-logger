# Store listing (en-US only)

**Title:** Notification Logger

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):**
Keep a private, on-device history of every notification.

**Full description:**
Never lose a notification again. Notification Logger keeps a searchable history of the notifications your phone shows, so you can read one you swiped away by accident.

• Logs notifications from every app, or only the apps you choose
• Browse by app, newest or oldest first
• See how a notification changed (download progress, updates) and hide repeats
• Copy text, or tap to reopen the original notification while it's recent
• Export to a text file whenever you like
• Clear history for one app or all, or delete old notifications automatically
• Optional fingerprint/PIN lock, and hidden from the recent-apps screen when locked

Private by design: no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Needs notification access, which you grant in Android settings.
